# Afterword

## Example (2021)
Afterword
Thank you for taking the time to read our first Sustainability Report. We have tried to broadly cover as many topics in relation to our ESG activities as possible, and we sincerely hope you have found the contents informative and enjoyable.
Having recently joined Unison’s Investor Solutions Team in the midst of the COVID-19 pandemic and being restricted to travel, editing this report has granted me the opportunity to travel to Sukesan’s headquarters to take part in the interview introduced earlier. The trip came with an opportunity for me to try Sukesan’s signature udon noodles for the first time, which was absolutely delicious, embodying all of the aspirations the first generation founder had for Sukesan to grow to a become a beloved company within its local community. Learning about all of the effort that goes behind preparing this local delicacy, for the soup being prepared early every morning to freshly made noodles being delivered from the factory to each restaurant, this valuable experience has allowed me to truly understand why Sukesan is perceived as a "soul food" of its local community, and I have become a fan myself. Most importantly, this experience has reminded me first hand of the responsibility we incur to the local community of our portfolio companies as private equity investors. 
The summer of 2021 has been quite a busy one for Unison, largely due to the joint vaccination programs catered to the Unison and CHCP Group and its portfolio companies introduced earlier, which has been a collaborative and firm-wide effort in every sense. It was an incredible experience to take part in, and amazing to witness the social impact that can be achieved with Unison, CHCP Group and our portfolio companies working hand in hand. The joint vaccination program came about after learning that Unison’s smaller portfolio companies (with less than 1,000 employees) were to be excluded from government-led workplace vaccination programs. Our intentions were simple – to be able to provide as many employees of our portfolio companies with vaccination opportunities that were otherwise not possible. Taking part in these operations and seeing the number of people who took advantage of the program to be vaccinated first hand, I am truly thankful to the colleagues who made this possible.
We plan to continue issuing our Sustainability Report annually, and cordially welcome any feedback or comments to improve the report. Lastly, please feel free to contact us should there be any topics of your interest to be covered in future reports.

Eri KawanaEditor

## Example (2022)
Afterword
Thank you for taking the time to read our second annual Sustainability Report. This year we have concentrated on two key topics – the progress we have made with RIMM to date, as well as Unison/CHCP’s efforts to resolve CNS-related healthcare issues through its investments. 

Unison and RIMM have been co-developing RIMM’s scoring report and dashboard since 2020. This year marked a significant step forward, with the RIMM assessment being implemented across Unison’s portfolio. This milestone would not be possible without the tireless efforts of all involved in the cycles of demos and pilot tests, followed by feedback and improvements. We are proud to be able to report to you the progress we have made to date in this report.  Editing this report has also been a great opportunity for me to learn about CNS field, which I was previously unfamiliar with. I began my journey by reading books about and studying the topic, only to learn early-on that average hospitalization periods in Japan for mental health conditions and neurological diseases that make up the CNS field are far greater than that of other countries. This challenge, along with many others unique to Japan, are exactly those which Unison and CHCP intend to address in its investments. Speaking to portfolio company management and medical practitioners about this topic, I was fascinated with the earnest passion driving these individuals to do better for the patients they serve. The experience has reminded me of the change and social impact we can promote as private equity investors. 

I sincerely hope you enjoyed this report, and cordially welcome any feedback or comments towards improving our future reports.

## Example (2023)
Afterword
Thank you for taking the time to read our third Unison Sustainability Report. Each year we are excited to share with you our progress on this initiative, and we are encouraged by the comments we receive from time to time that the contents are being well received.

This report focused on three special features. Two of these features highlight how Unison's portfolio companies are addressing the changing population dynamics in Japan, including Yuko Yuko’s work to engage and revitalize local communities as well as Kids Corporation’s support of dual-income households. The aging of Japan's population is not solely a negative phenomenon. It is essential for Japan's future development that healthy seniors continue to participate in society and engage in the economy. Kids Corporation, on the other hand, is working to play a key role in stemming the declining birthrate by supporting dual-income families. Continued social and economic development cannot be expected under the current situation, in which one of the spouses withdraws from the labor force to raise children or continues to work while carrying an excessive burden as a married couple with children. With dual income households increasingly becoming the norm, Unison will continue to support Kids Corporation's business expansion and efforts to increase the number of couples with children.

With investors often asking if we can quantitatively demonstrate Unison’s portfolio ESG activities, we have spent the past year collecting a variety of data from portfolio companies. However, when we started measuring GHG emissions, we made several discoveries, such as the fact that we could not determine the exact amount of electricity used at many stores located in commercial facilities, or the extent emissions were directly or indirectly related to the portfolio company. Each time this happened, I was painfully aware that I still had much to learn and was reminded once again that if I am going to ask a portfolio company to disclose data, I must also continue to expand my understanding to better promote the change and social impact we can help make as private equity investors.

I sincerely hope you enjoyed this report and cordially welcome any feedback or comments.

Eri Kawana, Editor

## Example (2024)
Afterword

This year marks the fourth edition of Unison's Sustainability Report, first published in 2021. Since our founding, Unison has been committed to a business purpose that goes beyond mere financial gain, encompassing social impact and value creation.

This year's report focuses on our consumer sector practice, which has continuously evolved over the 25 years since the establishment of Fund I. As demonstrated by the initiatives of Pyuru and Daytona International, we view ESG not merely as a set of rules or obligations, but as an integral part of our core business. Each portfolio company has implemented concrete measures aimed at benefiting local communities, employees, and the environment.

As these examples illustrate, our goal is to help the companies we invest in achieve both long-term enhancement of corporate value and fulfillment of their social responsibilities. To objectively assess the quantitative impact of these initiatives on society, we have co-developed a sustainability visualization tool with Rimm and participated in the EDCI, aiming to disseminate industry best practices across our portfolio.

This report also features photographs of wildflowers taken by Rahul Bhasin, a founding member of Wildflower Capital, as well as some photos I took myself at a botanical garden in Karuizawa.

Through this report, we aim to present concrete and tangible examples of ESG initiatives and provide a foundation for discussion on the role of ESG in the private equity business model. We welcome your feedback and insights.

Heuigon Song
Editor
