# Greeting
# Example (2021)
To our valued investors, 

We are pleased to hereby present to you our first Sustainability Report. I have been responsible for our “Impact Investment Initiative”, a recently redefined framework embodying our ESG-related activities. 

Unison’s investment strategy focused on control investments targeting small to mid-cap companies has remained unchanged since inception. As part of this strategy, strengthening governance and creating better environments for our portfolio companies, their employees and periphery have always been a central part of our value creation. Our first Sustainability Report revisits such efforts and reorganizes them to better suit our renewed ESG framework. In a time where ESG cannot be understated, preparing this report has been a great opportunity of self-reflection for us to recognize how far we have come, as well as humbly recognizing what more needs to be done. The recent expansion of our ESG-related ecosystem involving academia and third-party evaluators has been part of a conscious effort towards doing more in the future. Going forward, we strive to be able to objectively quantify the impact of these efforts within our investment activities by leveraging these relationships.  

On a personal note, employee empowerment has been a determining factor behind private equity being my career of choice. Upon joining the workforce, I was shocked to learn that a vast majority of the working population find their job as not much more than a means to make ends meet. I strongly felt that the influence private equity investors exercise on its portfolio companies can ultimately change this, increasing the number of people who find pride and joy in their work – this has continued to be a source of my motivation since. I sincerely believe that our commitment to ESG will play a pivotal role in driving value by bringing out the passion people find in their work. 

Lastly, this Sustainability Report is intended to reinforce our commitment to ESG – we hope to make steady progress going forward by continuing to approach ESG both genuinely and realistically. We warmly welcome any comments, suggestions or feedback from our readers. 

Junko Katayanagi
Partner

# Example (2022)
To our valued investors, 						

We are pleased to hereby present this year’s sustainability report. 
Included in our Guiding Principles, which were established at the time of Unison’s inception and serve at the core of our work as investors, is the phrase “We seek to create business transactions which have value not only to ourselves but also to society.” As our ESG framework evolves, we continue to keep this principle top of mind. At a group level, along with operating country-focused funds in both Japan and South Korea, impact investment activities in India have begun to take shape, leading to further strengthened capabilities to fund and support the growth of sustainability-oriented businesses across these regions as well as Singapore and marking an expansion of Unison’s business domain. 

A key goal with our flagship private equity funds’ investments continues to be to “Democratize meaningful work”. In a position to implement effective governance as shareholders supporting companies that serve society, Unison is able to shape portfolio companies’ work environments and ensure a motivated, thriving workforce. This in turn enhances organizational engagement and recruiting, which are linked to improved financial performance, and creates a positive feedback loop from “democratizing” the workplace to ultimately creating value and generating returns.

One highlight of our ESG efforts during the past year has been the expansion of the RIMM Sustainability Assessment to all of Unison’s portfolio companies. This has enabled us to examine each company’s individually set ESG efforts through a more quantitative, benchmark-oriented lens and make both competitor and portfolio-level comparisons. From 2022 we also began participating in ILPA’s ESG Data Convergence Initiative, and accordingly plan to aggregate annual changes in portfolio company data during Unison’s investment. Throughout these activities we continue to work to determine the best methodology for collecting small to mid-cap companies’ environmental impact data.

While Unison is still in the early stages of its ESG activities, another highlight of this past year has been on increasing opportunities to externally communicate our goals. World Economic Forum workshops, Private Equity International’s Responsible Investment Forum, Nikkei SDGs Festival, GZERO Summit are just some of the events where we’ve been provided opportunities to share our ESG framework and expand our network of likeminded organizations and individuals. Through these encounters and discussions with many corporate chief sustainability officers, we continue to build a community we can both learn from and share challenges with.

As always, we warmly welcome any comments, suggestions or feedback from our readers.
Junko Katayanagi


# Example (2023)
To our valued investors

We are pleased to hereby present this year’s sustainability report.

This year marks Unison’s 25th anniversary, which was founded in 1998. While Unison has continued to expand geographically with buyout funds in Korea and impact investments in India, Japan remains our core region with top priority placed on supporting value creation in a market with limited growth, a declining working population, and one of the world’s most aged societies. In this year’s report, we introduce examples of portfolio companies that are creating a direct impact in revitalizing local communities and supporting dual-income households. We are also pleased to report on our ESG data quantification efforts, which have finally reached a stage where the collected data can be analyzed.

Because of Japan’s changing demographics and maturing market, companies are increasingly expected to “innovate” and “increase organizational engagement” by improving operational efficiency and labor conditions. In conjunction with our digitalization and business infrastructure initiatives, this year we moved forward with improving labor conditions and diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) initiatives across our portfolio companies, with the aim to ensure each company can autonomously continue these efforts well after Unison's investment period ends. Promoting ESG is an integral part of our value creation efforts as a buyout investor, and we are also seeing firsthand the positive impact communicating portfolio companies’ environmental impact awareness has from hiring and marketing standpoints, which in turn leads to value creation and improved returns.

Looking back on the past year, the Japan Private Equity Association (“JPEA”) established its ESG Committee, which has promoted knowledge sharing and study sessions among GPs in Japan. For the first time, JPEA was able to collect ESG data and visually demonstrate that nearly 250,000 portfolio company jobs have been created in Japan and that portfolio companies’ DEI indicators are improving. I also became a member of PRI’s Japan Advisory Committee, which has given us more opportunities to exchange ideas with asset managers in the industry. PRI in Person will be held in Tokyo for the first time in October, with Unison slated to present at the event. We hope to continue to increase opportunities to communicate Japanese GPs’ efforts both in Japan and abroad.

As always, we warmly welcome any comments, suggestions, or feedback from our readers.

Junko Katayanagi
Partner

# Example (2024)
To our valued investors,

We are pleased to hereby present this year’s Sustainability Report, our fourth edition.

In this issue, we look back at Unison's journey so far. Steered by our Guiding Principle of making an impact on the business community through investment, we have steadily worked toward and achieved quantification and external sharing of our impact.


This year's report features two case studies in the consumer goods sector, where sustainability initiatives are now evaluated by consumers themselves and we believe that positive changes in the investment period directly contribute to increased corporate value. The first of these discusses Pyuru’s concrete commitments toward net-zero emissions and the system they have established, five years after our investment, enabling them to continue these initiatives independently even after Unison's exit. The second details our commitment to driving positive environmental change in the apparel industry, known for its significant impact in this area.


Unison has launched a long-planned direct impact investing strategy, alongside our initiatives to apply ESG in private equity. As we introduced in last year’s Sustainability Report, our first project as part of this strategy is a waste recycling and alternative fuels project in India. We are also planning to launch a fund in this sector and make more impact investments.


A further highlight of the past year was PRI in Person, held in Tokyo for the first time last October. The Japan Private Equity Association (JPEA) organized a side event to showcase the initiatives of Japanese GPs. This event was co-hosted with Initiative Climat International and provided valuable insights into leading GPs’ efforts to reduce greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions. We believe that private equity funds can drive positive change in portfolio companies, and that we should take this responsibility seriously.


As always, we warmly welcome any comments, suggestions, or feedback from our readers.


Junko Katayanagi
Partner
